# JustDropIt Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings:

**Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → JustDropIt**

Please note: Deleting the App does **not** cancel your subscription.

### Can I get a refund?
Payments are processed by Apple and are non-refundable except where required by law. To request a refund, please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How does the free quota work?
All users receive a free daily quota of photo-processing operations:
- **First use**: 200 photos (cumulative)
- **Daily**: 30 photos per day

Upgrade to **VIP** for unlimited access.

### Is my photo data safe?
Yes. All photo processing is performed **locally on your device**. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your photos to any external server.

### I accidentally deleted a photo. Can I recover it?
Photo deletion operations are **irreversible**. Please review carefully before confirming deletion. We are not liable for any loss of photos.

### What features are included with VIP?
- Unlimited daily photo processing
- AI-powered photo analysis
- Batch delete mode

### How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app and tap the VIP badge, then select "Restore Purchases". Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
