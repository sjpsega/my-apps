# Privacy Policy for JustDropIt

**Last updated: April 19, 2026**

We respect your privacy. "JustDropIt" is designed to be private by default.

## Data Collection
We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data on our servers.
- **Photos Data**: JustDropIt accesses your photo library solely for the purpose of displaying and managing photos on your device. All photo operations (Pick/Drop) are performed locally. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your photos to any external server.
- **Purchase Data**: In-app purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g., crash reports, logs) to improve the app, but this data cannot be used to identify you.

## Permissions
- **Photo Library Access**: JustDropIt requires access to your photo library to function. This permission is used exclusively to display photos and perform deletions you confirm. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide app functionality:

- **Firebase** (Google LLC): Analytics and crash reporting. [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **RevenueCat**: In-app purchase management and subscription status verification. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

These services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not share your photos or personal content with these services.

## Data Retention

Usage analytics data is retained for up to 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted or anonymized. Subscription-related data is retained as long as necessary to provide the service.

## Data Storage and Security

All photo processing occurs locally on your device. We do not maintain servers that store your photos or personal data.

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of this policy.
