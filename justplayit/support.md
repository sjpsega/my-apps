# JustPlayIt Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What media formats are supported?
JustPlayIt supports all formats natively decodable by AVPlayer:
- **Video**: mp4, mov, m4v (H.264 / H.265 / HEVC)
- **Audio**: mp3, m4a, wav, aac, flac

If a file cannot be played, it may use an unsupported codec or container format.

### How do I import videos or audio?
You can import media from two sources:
- **Photos**: Tap the "Photos" button to browse your photo library and select a video.
- **Files**: Tap the "Files" button to access iCloud Drive or local storage and select a video or audio file.

### How does Picture-in-Picture (PiP) work?
PiP activates automatically when you switch to another app while a video is playing. You can also manually trigger PiP by tapping the PiP button in the playback controls. The video will continue playing in a floating window.

### Can I play audio in the background?
Yes. When you lock your device or switch to another app, audio playback continues. You can control playback (play/pause, seek, skip) from the lock screen or Control Center.

### What speed options are available?
- **Free**: 1.0x (normal speed)
- **Pro**: 0.5x, 0.75x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x, 3.0x

Pro speed options are marked with a lock icon. Tapping a locked option will present the upgrade screen.

### How does the long-press fast forward work?
Long-press the screen during video playback to temporarily activate 2x speed. Release to return to the previous speed. This feature requires Pro unlock.

### How do I take a video screenshot?
While playing a video, use the screenshot button to capture the current frame. The screenshot is saved directly to your photo library. This feature requires Pro unlock and photo library write permission.

### What is included in Pro?
Pro is a **one-time purchase** that permanently unlocks:
- Variable speed playback (0.5x – 3.0x)
- Long-press temporary 2x fast forward
- Video screenshot (capture and save current frame)

There is no subscription and no recurring charges. Future Pro features will be included at no additional cost.

### How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open the App and access the Paywall, then tap **"Restore Purchases"**. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Your Pro status will be restored automatically.

### Can I get a refund?
Payments are processed by Apple and are non-refundable except where required by law. To request a refund, please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Is my media data safe?
Yes. All media playback occurs **locally on your device**. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files to any external server. We use Firebase for anonymous crash reporting and analytics only, without IDFA tracking.

### Where is my playback history?
JustPlayIt automatically records your recently played media in the "Recent" list on the home screen. Tap any item to resume playback. This data is stored locally on your device.
