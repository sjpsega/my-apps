# Privacy Policy for JustPlayIt

**Last updated: July 24, 2026**

We respect your privacy. "JustPlayIt" is designed to be private by default.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data on our servers.

- **Media Files**: JustPlayIt accesses your photo library and local files solely for the purpose of playing media on your device. All playback occurs locally. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files to any external server.
- **Purchase Data**: In-app purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.
- **Usage Data**: We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
- **Screenshots**: The Pro video screenshot feature saves captured frames directly to your photo library on your device. No screenshots are uploaded or transmitted.

## Permissions

- **Photo Library Access (Read)**: JustPlayIt requires read access to your photo library to import and play videos stored in Photos. This permission is used exclusively to browse and select media for playback. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.
- **Photo Library Access (Write)**: The Pro screenshot feature requires write access to save captured video frames to your photo library. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.
- **Files Access**: JustPlayIt uses the system document picker to access files from iCloud Drive or local storage. Access is limited to files you explicitly select.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide app functionality:

- **Firebase** (Google LLC): Anonymous analytics and crash reporting. We use Firebase Analytics without IDFA support, meaning your data is not used for advertising tracking. [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **RevenueCat**: In-app purchase management and purchase status verification. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

These services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not share your media files or personal content with these services.

## Data Retention

Anonymous usage analytics and crash report data is retained for up to 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted or anonymized. Purchase-related data is retained as long as necessary to verify and maintain your Pro entitlement.

## Data Storage and Security

All media playback and processing occurs locally on your device. We do not maintain servers that store your media files or personal data.

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of this policy.
